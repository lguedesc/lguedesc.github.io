[🇺🇸] The MECANON research group participated in the first Italian-Brazilian Workshop on Metamaterials and Smart Systems, held on August 21 at the National Laboratory for Scientific Computing ([LNCC](https://www.gov.br/lncc/pt-br)) in Petrópolis, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

The workshop brought together researchers from Brazil and Italy for a day of high-level scientific discussions, new ideas, and exchanges on metamaterials, smart systems, and related research topics.

The event was also an excellent opportunity to explore new research directions and identify promising opportunities for international collaborations.

We would like to thank the organizing committee [Americo Cunha](https://www.linkedin.com/in/americocunhajr/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D), [Bruno Cesar Cayres](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brunoccayres/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D), [Daduí C. Guerrieri](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dadui/en/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D), and [Fernando Fraternali](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fernando-fraternali-50017756/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D) for the invitation, and all the research partners who contributed to making this event possible.

We look forward to the collaborations and ideas that will emerge from this meeting! 🚀

---

[🇧🇷] Nosso grupo de pesquisa participou do primeiro Italian-Brazilian Workshop on Metamaterials and Smart Systems, realizado no dia 21 de agosto no Laboratório Nacional de Computação Científica ([LNCC](https://www.gov.br/lncc/pt-br)), em Petrópolis, Rio de Janeiro, Brasil.

O workshop reuniu pesquisadores do Brasil e da Itália para um dia de discussões científicas de alto nível, troca de ideias e experiências sobre metamateriais, sistemas inteligentes e temas de pesquisa relacionados.

O evento também foi uma excelente oportunidade para explorar novas linhas de pesquisa e identificar perspectivas promissoras de colaborações internacionais.

Agradecemos à comissão organizadora: [Americo Cunha](https://www.linkedin.com/in/americocunhajr/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D), [Bruno Cesar Cayres](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brunoccayres/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D), [Daduí C. Guerrieri](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dadui/en/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D), and [Fernando Fraternali](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fernando-fraternali-50017756/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_detail_base%3Bm3SIrIFhTkea15a0NfrvkA%3D%3D) pelo convite, e a todos os parceiros de pesquisa que contribuíram para tornar esse evento possível.

Estamos ansiosos pelas colaborações e ideias que surgirão a partir desse encontro! 🚀
