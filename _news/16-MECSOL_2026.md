This week, I had the pleasure of attending **MECSOL 2026**, one of the leading international symposia in solid mechanics regularly held in Brazil. The event took place from October 19 to 21 at the [Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica - ITA](https://www.ita.br) in São José dos Campos, São Paulo, Brazil.

It was an excellent opportunity to present our preliminary work on **mechanical energy harvesting using origami-based structures**, as well as to reconnect with colleagues, exchange ideas, and establish new research collaborations.
